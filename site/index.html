Speaking Mastery إِتْقَانُ التَّحَدُّثِ

One studio for every lesson. Practise the key language, perform exam-style role plays, develop topic-conversation answers at Core, Develop or Stretch level, and sit a timed mock speaking exam — with your own recordings to compare against model answers.

Works best in Microsoft Edge or Chrome (natural Arabic voices and the pronunciation check). Safari works too. Allow the microphone when asked — recordings never leave your device.

1Key languageHear it, say it, record it.
2Role playsCards A–D, including the unexpected “!” task.
3ConversationThink time, a speaking timer and model answers with a range spotter.
4Mock examTimed, recorded, self-assessed out of 12.
5My progressBadges, a to-do list and mock scores.

For students

Pick your route (Core, Develop or Stretch) at the top of each lesson. Rate every answer honestly — “Again” answers appear in your to-do list. Your progress is saved in this browser on this device.

For teachers

Share a lesson in Teams with computer sound on, and run the role plays as a class: the studio voices the examiner while pupils answer. ⎙ Print gives you cue cards and model answers for breakout rooms.

Classroom routine (15 min)

3 min key language shadowing → 5 min role play in pairs → 5 min conversation with the timer → 2 min self-rating. Set the mock exam as homework and ask for the downloaded recording.
